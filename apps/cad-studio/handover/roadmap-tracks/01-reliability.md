## Scope and status

P0 addresses the package and Studio boundaries identified by D519. At D520 every entry started with runtime reproduction NOT_RUN. The current roadmap owns present delivery and next-item status; each queue row below links its bounded closeout when available. Those records distinguish controlled failure, actual consumer proof and remaining unknowns. Existing passing workflows remain valid only at their recorded source and scope.

Work in the order below. First reproduce the selected boundary without changing its inputs. If it cannot be reproduced, record the supported explanation and exact remaining unknown; do not invent a fix. A concrete data-loss or wrong-geometry failure takes precedence over breadth work in P1–P6.

## Closeout and reporting

Each R item needs its own decision, original reproduction or documented non-reproduction, selected tier, affected checks, current-source ownership and one relevant actual desktop Studio flow. R08 additionally needs the actual React/Viewer consumer. Reuse prior passing evidence only when its input/source boundary is eligible. Preserve original failures; source hashes establish custody, not numerical correctness.

Report PASS, FAIL and NOT_RUN separately. Mark an item accepted only for its declared case matrix, with linked evidence. The twelve R IDs are an initial reliability backlog, not the denominator of whole CAD completion. Broader P1–P6 work can proceed only where it does not depend on an unresolved P0 boundary; do not wait for a held or unavailable external dependency.
