The copied native protocol, proof specification, prices, limits, worker, payload/cost gates and independent Python checker remain unchanged.

GCD first normalizes signs and may interchange its two nonnegative operands. For a sorted positive pair with more than64 leading bits, it takes a shared64-bit leading window. It builds at most64 quotient steps of a2x2 integer matrix. The proposed quotients are `floor((x+A)/(y+C))` and `floor((x+B)/(y+D))`. Both numerators must be nonnegative, both denominators positive, both quotients equal and positive, and the chosen quotient and all retained matrix coefficients must have magnitude at most2^64-1. Each step left-multiplies the matrix by `[[0,1],[1,-q]]`.

Before accepting any block, at least two steps must have completed. The full exact determinant must be+1 or-1. The full transformed integers must satisfy `aNew>=0`, `bNew>=0`, `aNew<=aOld` and `bNew<bOld`. An integer unimodular transform preserves the gcd, and the strict decrease of nonnegative b proves termination. These exact checks establish correctness even if the leading estimate was unhelpful. A rejected or unavailable block performs ONE ordinary Euclid remainder step. This is one GCD algorithm, not a solver fallback, alternate basis, tolerance or answer repair. No approximate quotient itself is accepted as a proof result.

No full transform is attempted when `a_bits+65>16385`:64 bits cover a coefficient product and the extra bit covers adding two signed products. This also satisfies the required `a_bits+64` exclusion. Every large operation uses the original checked wrappers. The small leading invariant proves corrected sums/quotients fit65bits, products fit128bits and differences fit129bits. Small helpers reserve the original operation price and prebound BEFORE allocation. They omit a BIT_LENGTH inspection only when its proven result bound is already below an actually measured global peak. Otherwise the original checked operation inspects the actual value. Thus the exact peak remains exact; no actual inspection or arithmetic operation is unpriced. The initial leading values and every retained matrix coefficient are admitted before these bounds are used.

`gcdRemainders` counts actual GCD REM operations, not estimated skipped steps or quotient operations. Every matrix, quotient, sign, determinant and progress computation uses original-priced operations. No production proof counter fields are added. The per-call1024-key bit cache and all8192/16385/2M limits remain unchanged.
