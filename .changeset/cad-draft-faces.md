Draft on chosen faces: a part's `draft` feature turns named side faces (planar, square to Z) about their line on the neutral plane (default the body's bottom) by an angle — in takes material off, out adds it — for faces that do not meet each other (adjacent ones are refused with the reason). The polyhedral Boolean skips a region of no area instead of failing on it.
