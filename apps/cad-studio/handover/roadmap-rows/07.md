## Scope and ownership

- Scope: Cover advanced modeling families: guided loft, fillets, editable text/relief, threads and cylindrical wrap.
- Owner and dependencies: Kernel/Model then Studio; general rational, multi-span and frame extensions need separate scope.

## Recorded acceptance requirement

The following is the original row acceptance requirement, not a fresh verification claim:

> A new extension needs its own bounded input, original tolerances, geometry/mass oracle and actual editable Studio/file proof. No whole-family or release acceptance is implied by the delivered profiles.
