P0 addresses the package and Studio boundaries identified by D519. At D520 every entry started with runtime reproduction NOT_RUN. Those records distinguish controlled failure, actual consumer proof and remaining unknowns. Existing passing workflows remain valid only at their recorded source and scope.

Work in the order below. First reproduce the selected boundary without changing its inputs. If it cannot be reproduced, record the supported explanation and exact remaining unknown; do not invent a fix. A concrete data-loss or wrong-geometry failure takes precedence over breadth work in P1–P6.
