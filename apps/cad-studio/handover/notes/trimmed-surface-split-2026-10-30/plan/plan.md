## Decision

Recommend functional **trim-preserving parameter Split** for one original affine support and one simple stored3..160-point loop. Reuse existing native support splitting and existing exact segment arrangement; add a bounded adapter for material components, certified storage rounding and parent-to-child binding. This is not another guard, a new Part solid verb, a B-rep sewing/Boolean engine, or a rebuild of source copies. It closes the specific D336 guarded consumer after D337 Reverse and D338/D339 snapshot facades.

Generic multiple loops, curved supports, arbitrary cutting curves, solid mass and repeated-operation closure remain separate scopes.08 remains before09; delivered07 and queued Electron15 do not change.

## Parent lineage and whole-child world binding

Do not call ExactAffinePlane on each native child and pretend insertion preserves its exact fourth-pole affine identity. Native `curve-knot-insertion.ts:101-120` computes represented alpha, homogeneous products/sums and dehomogenization; Surface insertion applies it per strip. Even original affine equal-weight support can yield tiny nonaffine/weight differences. Existing parent observations≤128 do not prove the whole child.

Use original affine authority and existing native split provenance/domain maps to certify each actual child over its whole rectangle:

1. From original four exact stored poles and original domains, construct the exact original affine restriction A(s,t) to each child's actual domain (s,t in[0,1]). This has four bilinear Bernstein control values; do not round them into a replacement support.
2. Read actual child's Cartesian poles Qij and positive weights wij. Its exact represented coefficient model is H(s,t)/W(s,t), with H coefficients wij*Qij and W coefficients wij. Do not assume equal child weight bits.
3. Form R = elevate(H) - W*A in the **fixed degree2x2 Bernstein basis**, using exact rational multiplication/elevation (only9 coefficients per Cartesian axis). The existing section exact-binding implementation already uses exact Bernstein product/elevation and convex-hull residual logic (`features/section-exact-surface-binding.ts:74-105`); reuse a small internal scalar utility or an explicitly bounded nine-coefficient formula, not that whole unrelated section public producer.
4. Because Bernstein weights are nonnegative and W >= min(wij)>0, a conservative Euclidean bound is `Bchild = max_k(sum_xyz(abs(Rk_xyz))) / min(wij)`, outward-rounded. This is a whole-child coefficient-model position bound against original affine authority, even if the actual child is slightly bilinear/rational. It is not global binary64 evaluator error or surface embedding certification.
5. For exact arrangement vertex p rounded to stored UV p', bound original affine displacement as `|du|*Lu + |dv|*Lv`, where Lu/Lv are exact L1 world derivative scales from original poles/domain spans. Require **Bchild + Buv <= original linear1e-7**, plus each UV rounding displacement<=original UV1e-7. Do not independently spend1e-7 twice. This sum bounds whole corresponding boundary segments by convexity; the bound is not just endpoint sampling.
6. Also retain parent native observations/report unmodified, and perform local original/actual-child native evaluator comparisons at the stored trim/cut vertices within1e-7, reported separately. These observations supplement the coefficient bound. No exact planar child, embedding, manifold or solid-mass claim follows.
