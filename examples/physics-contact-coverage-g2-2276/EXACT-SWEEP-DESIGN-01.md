## Candidate: piecewise closest-distance polynomial

Write x(t)=start+t*(end-start) relative to the box center. Use the original box half extents, not half+r. The squared distance is the sum, per coordinate, of the squared outside gap to[-half,+half]. It is continuous and piecewise quadratic.

1. Check the initial closest-point squared distance. At or inside r² returns INITIAL_DISCRETE_OWNED, including touching, overlap and center-inside-box. Zero displacement outside returns MISS. No expanded-box initial exclusion is used.
2. For each moving coordinate form its two plane-crossing times. Keep only strict0<t<1; append0/1; sort, removing exact duplicate numbers only. At most6 internal breaks give at most7 nonempty intervals. No epsilon merge or time clamp.
3. At the midpoint of each interval identify the coordinates below/above their original box bounds. Their signed gaps have the form alpha_i+beta_i*t. In axis order form A=sum(beta²), B=2sum(alpha*beta), C=sum(alpha²)-r². Positions, closest-point tests, center interpolation and emitted hit values use fixed documented operation order. Native coefficients and every root decision are captured in test-only case receipts.
4. Inspect intervals chronologically, including closed boundaries. If the left boundary itself is within r², it is a hit. If A=0 and B=0, the constant-distance interval is either outside or a contact plateau; its first legal boundary wins. A=0/B!=0 uses the linear root without division by A; this branch is mathematical completeness and is not claimed covered when no frozen case reaches it.
5. For A>0, compute D=B²-4AC. D<0 has no quadratic root; D=0 uses -B/(2A). For D>0 use q=-0.5*(B+copysign(sqrt(D),B)), with the explicitly fixed B=0 sign choice positive; roots q/A and C/q. If q=0 use the double-root expression. Choose the earliest finite root inside the current closed interval and[0,1]. Never turn a negative discriminant positive, snap/clamp an out-of-domain root, average roots, or expand a bound to obtain a hit.
6. Recompute the closest point at the selected fraction. The nonzero center-to-closest-point vector gives the fixture-to-sphere normal, normalized with one Math.hypot. A nonfinite/zero normal is NUMERIC_LIMIT, not a fabricated face normal. Edge/vertex feature ties use the unique Euclidean closest point; no normal averaging. Sort ties deterministically by time, interval index and fixed axis/feature order without altering the fraction.

Native sign/rounding decisions must pass the independent gates below. Stable roots reduce cancellation; they do not prove every floating-point degeneracy. An uncertain or wrong classification is a first failure. Do not add a discriminant epsilon, retries, higher iteration count or a wider profile after observing it.

## Independent reference: boundary features, not interval code

The stdlib Python reference never imports candidate/Physics code or shares its interval/active-set builder. It decodes canonical F64 words exactly as Fraction. It enumerates the boundary of box plus Euclidean sphere by geometric features:6 offset face patches,12 edge cylinders and8 vertex spheres.

Face roots come from their plane equations and must have their other coordinates inside the original face rectangle. Edge roots come from squared distance to the infinite supporting edge line; the free coordinate must lie on its finite segment, and the two outward coordinates must be in the edge's exterior quadrant. Vertex roots come from squared distance to that vertex with all three coordinates in its outward octant. All feature restrictions and0<=t<=1 are closed. Initial closest-point distance independently decides INITIAL_DISCRETE_OWNED. No valid feature root plus an initially outside state proves MISS by continuity. Tangencies and endpoint roots are retained; a sign-change-only search is not used.

Exact rational polynomial coefficients, including linear/constant degeneracies, are evaluated independently from feature geometry. Exact square detection uses bounded integer isqrt on numerator/denominator; rational roots and feature-boundary equalities are decided exactly. Otherwise, directed Decimal80/120 square-root/root enclosures are fixed before execution. Check endpoint squares with counted exact arithmetic. Width must be <=1e-65 at80 and <=1e-105 at120;120 must lie inside80. No adaptive precision or iterative fallback.

Exact rational duplicates are collapsed. Equal same-branch roots of proportional quadratics can be identified by exact coefficient cross-products. If two remaining enclosures overlap and common-root/feature validity cannot be proved within the frozen arithmetic, report UNRESOLVED and stop; do not choose by an epsilon. The selected root certificate includes the feature equation, domain constraints, earliest-selection evidence, both precision enclosures, actual Context settings/flags and independently computed closest-point normal. Native initial rounding is separate from intended rational literals.

The reference can enumerate at most46 algebraic candidates/case:6 face roots,24 edge roots and16 vertex roots. At most20 quadratic discriminants/case need square-root enclosures. Only the selected/necessary candidate proofs are retained; rejected candidates still have counted classification and durable bounded proof records. No bisection, ODE, linear solver, trig, extra trajectory or old-helper replay is needed.
