Native surface viewer

Two source-version-0 BaroCAD surfaces. The mesh and hit are display approximations; the native model stays available.

Click a surface to select it. Coordinates are right-handed Y-up, source millimetres, displayed at 0.001 scene units per mm.

Native Surface points

Enter original U/V parameters, or choose a slot and click a displayed triangle. A picked U/V is approximate until the native Surface is evaluated.
Native Curve chord
Curves stay in the native model and are not drawn. Enter original parameters. The result is a straight chord, not Curve arc length.
Native Surface-to-Curve distance
Enter original Surface U/V and Curve t. The Curve is not drawn. This measures a straight line between native points, not a path along either geometry.
