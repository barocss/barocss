## Delta from the previous eight-job map

The additional needs are: (1) Studio host-visible GPU recovery, (2) a documented public acceptance/Undo/cancel identity across existing immutable and candidate mechanisms, (3) computation-scoped Stop proof, (4) mass-method/cause/estimate semantics reaching the actual decision, (5) measured session-resource admission, and (6) a clearly selected native measurement metric. These are narrower than new CAD feature families. Several are evidence gates, not missing implementation.

No newly verified absent commercial feature is asserted unconditionally. In particular, recovery/cancel/Undo/measurement/capability reporting already exist in important paths. The inspected gaps are exact host wiring, scope, and proof boundaries. A targeted job that already passes should close its gate with evidence and produce no issue.
