About Us

Sweet Haven Bakery has been serving our neighbourhood for over a decade with passion and quality ingredients. Every item is handcrafted daily by our skilled bakers using traditional recipes and the finest locally-sourced ingredients.

We believe that great bread and pastries bring joy to people's lives. Visit us to experience the warmth of our shop and the taste of authentic baking.
