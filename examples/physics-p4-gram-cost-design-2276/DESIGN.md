## Why this is a new algebra boundary

High reviewed pair-local coefficient arithmetic, not a result cache. The proposal computes AA/BB/AB Gram coefficients and signed EA·B, A·EB and (delta×A)·B tables for ONE pair and ONE raw-or-normalized convention. Discard them before the next pair/convention. There are no ID/axis keys, cache hits, Decimal result entries, weighted-radius reuse, new cross-pair/body/stage/request coefficient state, PRE/HYP coefficient reuse or Node SAT delegation. Existing qualified stage-local prepared-body structures remain unchanged; they do not authorize a new coefficient cache. These are fixed local common subexpressions inside a new exact algebraic evaluator; all actual new arithmetic remains metered.

Raw bases are arbitrary represented bases within existing admission. Do not replace AA/BB by identity or assume orthogonality, unit length or normalized raw quaternions. Normalized and raw conventions remain separately evaluated. All15 axes retain original order and all exact-zero/near-parallel/sign/first-minimum/tie classifications. Lagrange/determinant identities can produce larger intermediate products even where the original direct expression fits. The original16384-bit prebound may then refuse the new algorithm; preserve that failure. No Cartesian fallback, hidden uncapped calculation, approximate epsilon, division rescaling or operation-cap increase may seek a PASS. The critical B cofactor orientation is Bj×Bk, not Bk×Bj. Absolute NDS results alone can hide a global sign error, so signed controls are required.
