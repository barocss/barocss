## Global coupled equations, not two independent impacts

Dynamic vertical coordinates are `[lower,upper]`, mass matrix `M=diag(2,2)`. Floor and interface constraint rows are `A0=[1,0]`, `A1=[-1,1]`. Constants come from actual authored floor top and half-extents: `y_lower>=floorTop+half_lower`, `y_upper-y_lower>=half_lower+half_upper`. This is a two-DOF unilateral chain with a prescribed static boundary.

At each actual BEFORE, exact dyadic input gives `v_free=v_before+g*h` and `y_free=y_before+v_free*h`. Free-position reconstruction includes floating arithmetic residual because public state does not expose private rounded free positions. The reference first solves the strictly convex **global position projection** `min (y-y_free)^T M (y-y_free)/2` subject to both geometry constraints. It enumerates the four fixed active sets with scalar/2×2 Gram closed forms, checks nonnegative duals and all inequalities and selects the unique least objective. It does not reproduce Gauss-Seidel pair corrections, cold native QP or a general optimizer. Degenerate zero-multiplier sets may share the same unique target. A prefix with no active velocity constraints uses free motion.

The binding geometry rows of this independent position optimum define the end-step e0 normal contact constraints. The velocity reference solves `min (v-v_free)^T M (v-v_free)/2`, `A_active v>=0`, using the same four literal active sets. Before upper contact, only lower/floor constrains velocity; upper's free fall remains nonzero and is also checked against the independent n-step kick-drift closed form (free v1e-11). Once both constraints bind, the global projection itself may prove both velocities0. Zero is not imposed from a stationary whitelist or copied exact equilibrium output.

Public pair aggregates supply actual normal multipliers. The oracle uses actual A/B normal Y/sign to reconstruct their measured J coefficients. Ideal-axis reference multipliers are rescaled by the actual positive normal coefficient when comparing public normal Ns. Full vector body momentum and actual point/COM torque are checked separately, including any tangential or angular residue.
