🌍 Single-Origin Spotlight: Ethiopian Yirgacheffe

September 2026

This month we're featuring a fresh micro-lot from the highlands of Yirgacheffe. Harvested in July at 2,000 meters elevation, these beans showcase bright citrus notes with hints of jasmine and bergamot. Our roasters have crafted a light-medium roast that highlights the origin's natural complexity. Perfect for pour-over or espresso—grab a bag before it's gone.

💚 Our Commitment to Sustainability

Community Update

We're proud to announce that 100% of our coffee is now sourced through direct trade partnerships, ensuring fair compensation for our farmers.
