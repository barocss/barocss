Our Little Bakery

Flour & Hearth started with one wood-fired oven and a simple idea: good bread takes time, and time is worth taking. Every loaf is mixed, shaped, and baked right here by our small team.

We use local flour, natural leaven, and no shortcuts — just flour, water, salt, and patience. Stop by early; the best things sell out fast.
