Brew Tip

Dial In Before You Give Up

If your coffee tastes sour, it’s likely under-extracted — grind finer. Sharp and bitter? Go coarser. Change just one variable at a time and taste after every adjustment. Most “bad beans” are simply a grinder set two clicks off. Start at a 1:16 ratio (say, 20g coffee to 320g water) and let your palate guide the rest.
