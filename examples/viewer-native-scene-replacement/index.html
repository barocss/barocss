About local original files

Choose a local Source0 file, then select Open. Choosing or discarding a file does not change accepted geometry. Open uses the whole original source in mm and displays all one to four admitted Surface or Curve objects with a fitted view. Files must contain 1 to 65,536 UTF-8 bytes. The filename and extension do not determine the geometry. Unsupported or invalid files leave the accepted original scene in place when presentation recovery is available. This opens native geometry, without Face topology, a saved document, Shell, assembly or editing history. It does not upload or save your file.
